# 🐾 Pawtopia – A Better World for Every Paw

### Stray Dog & Cat Management and Rabies Control Platform

**A better world for every paw. **

## 🌍 About Pawtopia

Pawtopia is a smart, technology-driven animal welfare platform designed to improve the safety, health, and well-being of stray dogs and cats.

It provides a centralized digital ecosystem that connects citizens, NGOs, veterinarians, and municipal authorities to manage stray animal populations, monitor vaccination records, report emergencies, and coordinate rescue and sterilization activities.

By combining GPS-based tracking, QR-powered animal identification, AI-assisted image recognition, and data analytics, Pawtopia aims to make stray animal management more efficient, transparent, and accessible.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Stray dogs and cats often face challenges due to the lack of a centralized animal management system.

- No centralized database to track stray animal populations.
- Difficulty monitoring rabies vaccination records.
- Delayed reporting and rescue of injured animals.
- Poor coordination between citizens, NGOs, and authorities.
- Lack of proper sterilization tracking.
- Duplicate animal registrations and fragmented health records.

## 💡 Our Solution

Pawtopia brings all essential stray animal welfare activities into one integrated platform, enabling communities and animal welfare organizations to identify, monitor, protect, and care for stray animals.

## ✨ Key Features

**📍 Smart Stray Mapping**
- GPS-based tracking of stray dogs and cats.
- Interactive map with animal locations.
- Color-coded vaccination and health status.
- Location-based animal search and filtering.

**🏷️ QR-Based Health Identity**
- Unique QR code for every registered animal.
- Quick access to animal health records.
- Vaccination history and booster information.
- Sterilization status verification.

**💉 Vaccination Management**
- Digital anti-rabies vaccination records.
- Vaccination date and batch tracking.
- Upcoming booster schedule monitoring.
- Identification of animals requiring vaccination.

**🤖 AI-Assisted Animal Recognition**
- Image-based animal identification.
- Coat-pattern recognition to assist re-identification.
- Duplicate registration detection support.

**🚨 Emergency Reporting**
- Report injured, sick, or vulnerable animals.
- Submit location-based emergency reports.
- Prioritize rescue requests.
- Connect citizens with animal welfare teams.

**🏥 NGO & Municipal Coordination**
- Centralized rescue management dashboard.
- Assign rescue and vaccination tasks.
- Monitor field operations.
- Track activities through a Kanban workflow.

**🐕 Sterilization Tracking**
- Maintain sterilization records.
- Monitor sterilization coverage.
- Identify animals requiring sterilization.
- Support responsible population management.

**📊 Analytics Dashboard**
- Stray animal population statistics.
- Vaccination coverage monitoring.
- Sterilization progress tracking.
- Ward-wise animal distribution.
- Rabies prevention progress insights.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Mapping:** Leaflet, OpenStreetMap
- **AI & Image Recognition:** Python
- **QR Technology:** QR Code Generation
- **Authentication:** Role-Based Access Control
- **Data Visualization:** Chart.js
